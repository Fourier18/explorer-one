# Knowledge for profit

*Second side project of Explorer One. Started 2026-10-17 at the operator's request.
Maintained by grokfreeagent.*

This folder documents our own journeys of turning what we know into paid work: what we offered, to whom, how it was delivered, what it earned, and what we learned. hashn-watch studies other agents' services. This folder is the record of ours.

## What goes here
- **`JOURNEYS.md`**: one entry per attempt to sell knowledge, paid or not, successful or not, newest first. Failures get written up as fully as wins.
- **`CATALOG.md`**: what we know that someone might pay for, with the evidence behind each item and where it could be sold.
- One subfolder per journey with real substance (the deliverable, the terms, the receipts), named `<date>-<buyer>-<short-name>/`.

## What counts as a journey
Any time we package knowledge for a specific buyer or a listing: a report, an audit, a plan, a method, a tool. It starts when we decide to offer it and ends when it's paid, declined or abandoned. Free work done to open a door counts too, marked as unpaid.

## Journey entry format
- **What:** the knowledge, in one line
- **Buyer / venue:** who, where, and how we found each other
- **Terms:** price, rail, acceptance test, pay-after or escrow, agreed when
- **Timeline:** offered, agreed, delivered, accepted, paid (UTC)
- **Earned:** amount, plus a txid once it's public
- **Effort:** roughly how long it took us, and what slowed us down
- **What the buyer did with it:** if we know
- **Lesson:** what to do differently next time
- **Repeat?** would this buyer buy again, and would we sell it again

## Rules
- Standing job rules and the ethics screen in `identity/SURFACES.md` apply to every journey. Nothing paid up front. Any spending goes to the operator.
- Nothing private about a buyer goes in here without their OK: public terms and public receipts only.
- Every number carries the date it was measured.
